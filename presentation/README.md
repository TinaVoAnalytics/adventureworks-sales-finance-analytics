# Business Analysis Presentation

This folder contains the management-focused presentation developed for the **AdventureWorks Sales Finance & Profitability Analytics** project.

## Presentation Focus

The presentation translates the technical analysis into a business and management perspective, connecting the key findings to:

- Revenue growth versus profitability deterioration
- Product and subcategory profitability drivers
- Below-cost pricing exposure
- Promotion and discount diagnostics
- Pricing and cost sensitivity analysis
- Business risks and diagnostic hypotheses
- Corrective levers and management considerations
- Continuous profitability monitoring and decision support

## Analytical Story

**Business Problem → Financial Evidence → Profitability Drivers → Diagnostic Analysis → Sensitivity Analysis → Corrective Levers → Management Recommendations → Continuous Monitoring**

## Presentation File

`AdventureWorks_Sales_Profitability_Business_Analysis_Tina_Vo.pptx`

> The presentation is designed to demonstrate analytical storytelling by translating SQL, Excel, and Power BI findings into concise, management-focused business insights and decision-support considerations.
